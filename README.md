## 🔍 Key Features & Business Insights

* **Executive Overview:** High-level tracking for Total Revenue, Successful Transactions, Success Rate %, and Target Revenue Achievement.
* **Operational Channel Volume:** Funnel analysis across POS, Mobile App, QR, Web, and Call Center channels.
* **Revenue Drivers:** Breakdown of revenues by Payment Methods (Cash, Cards, Wallets) and Service Categories (Financial, Telecom, Utilities).
* **Monthly Revenue Variance:** Waterfall chart tracking cumulative monthly growth and seasonal peaks.
* **Regional & Merchant Analytics:** Interactive matrix and treemap analyzing top merchants and governorate contributions.
